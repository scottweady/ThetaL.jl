Symbols, filter and resolution

The stiff symbol

The small-scale decomposition of Hou, Lowengrub & Shelley: at high wavenumbers a velocity built from the curve's own geometry relaxes the tangent angle at a rate that grows with the wavenumber, $\theta_t \approx \sigma_k\hat\phi_k$ with $\sigma_k < 0$. The caller gives that growth rate as a function of the wavenumber along the curve, $q = 2\pi|k|/L$: symbol = q -> σ, or symbol = (q, X) -> σ when it depends on the curve X. It is the dispersion relation of the velocity linearized about a straight interface. The step evaluates it once per curve at the start of each step and holds the rates for the step. Whatever part of the velocity the symbol does not capture stays in the explicit remainder, so the symbol need not be exact: the step converges at its order with a wrong symbol too (Method overview, panel (d)), but steps that are large compared with $1/|\sigma|$ need the stiff part captured.

A symbol that depends on the curve must bound the stiffness it stands for (anisotropy: the largest value of $\gamma + \gamma''$ on the curve, examples/anisotropy.jl). A positive $\sigma$ makes the exponential amplify that mode; ThetaL warns. A rate that is not a finite real number is an error.

Measuring the symbol

measure_symbol(problem) measures the rates of the problem's velocity on one curve (the others held): for each mode $k$ it perturbs $\phi$ by $\pm\varepsilon\cos k\alpha$ and $\pm\varepsilon\sin k\alpha$ ($L$ and $x_0$ kept), forms $\theta_t$ as the step does (rigid part out), and reads the rate $\lambda_k = \langle p, Jp\rangle/\langle p, p\rangle$ of $p = e^{ik\alpha}$ from the central differences (four evaluations per mode; the velocity sees ev.kind = :probe). On a circle this is the exact linearization (curve shortening $2 - q^2$ on the unit circle, surface diffusion $q^2 - q^4$); on other curves the mode average, and with windows = m the rate of Gaussian packets at $m$ places along the curve, which a varying stiffness needs. An imaginary part is a transport rate, left explicit.

check_symbol(problem; dt) compares. Per mode the step treats $\sigma$ exactly and $(\lambda - \sigma)\hat\phi$ explicitly, and multiplies $\hat\phi$ by $G(\sigma h, (\lambda-\sigma) h)$ (from the scheme's weights). For ETDRK2, $G = e^{z} + w(\phi_1 - 2\phi_2)(z) + 2w\phi_2(z)\,(e^{z/2} + \tfrac{w}{2}\phi_1(z/2))$ with $z = \sigma h$, $w = (\lambda - \sigma)h$; at large steps $G \to r + 2r^2$, $r = \lambda/\sigma - 1$, so the step is stable at every $\Delta t$ only for $0 \le \lambda/\sigma \le 3/2$ (ExpEuler: $G \to -r$, $0 \le \lambda/\sigma \le 2$). A weaker symbol leaves a limit $\Delta t \sim 1/|\lambda|$ at the mode; the check reports, per mode, the largest step with $|G| \le \max(1, e^{\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda h})$, and warns when that is below dt (without dt: when any measured mode has one). The step bears it out to a few percent (test/testsymbolcheck.jl).

The spectral filter

Every scheme carries a SpectralFilter(; order = 36, cutoff = 0): mode $k$ is multiplied by $\exp(-36\,(|k|/k_c)^{\mathrm{order}})$ and modes $|k| \ge k_c$ are removed, on $\phi$ after every stage and on the deformation part of $U$ before it is differentiated. cutoff = 0 means $k_c = N/2$; an integer sets it; :auto sets it once, after the first velocity evaluation, from noise_cutoff: a computed velocity carries an error about flat in $k$, which the step damps at the rate of the symbol, so the noise settles at $\hat\phi_k \approx (2\pi/L) k \varepsilon/|\sigma_k|$; the cutoff is placed where the curve's spectrum meets that floor. Set it through the scheme: ETDRK2(filter_cutoff = :auto).

Resolution

The tail of a curve's spectrum (spectral_tail: the largest $|\hat\phi_k|/N$ around $k = N/2$) measures how well N resolves it. from_curve picks the smallest power of two from 64 whose tail is below tol/100. evolve and solve double N whenever the tail exceeds tol (up to Nmax), and, with coarsen = true, halve it once the tail on half the points has stayed below tol/100 for ten steps; the gap between the two thresholds and the wait keep them from undoing each other. One step over tol is enough to double N, and without coarsen it stays doubled, so a computed velocity whose error spikes now and then can push N up for good: ETDRK2(filter_cutoff = :auto) damps that noise (above), and coarsen = true brings N back. verbose = true prints each change of N with its time and cause. The default Nmax = 16384 is far beyond what an O(N²) velocity solve can afford; set it. With the integrator, double_grid! and halve_grid! change N between steps (the velocities are interpolated or truncated with the curve), on whatever rule the caller keeps.
